Getting started

  1. Add an item. Use Add item (the + in the middle of the bottom bar on phones). Paste a product link and the title and price you copied, or type them in.
  2. Mark it a Need or a Want and, if you like, give it a target price, a need-by date and a status such as Saving or Ready to buy.
  3. Plan your buying. Tick items to compare them side by side, change several at once, or open their stores one after another with Buy selected.
  4. Mark it purchased when you buy it. It moves to Purchased with what you spent; “Not buying” keeps a record of what you decided against.

Your list

Can I pull a product’s photo or price automatically from a link?

No. Nothing is fetched from the link you paste. Copy the title and price off the product page, paste them into the “paste copied title & price” box, or upload a photo yourself and fill in the fields by hand.

Does it track prices automatically?

No. Everything runs in your browser with no server, so it can’t check store prices in the background. When you update an item’s price, the old one is kept in its price history, and the card shows the lowest price you’ve recorded and whether it went up or down.

What are the small store labels on my items?

When a link matches a store this app recognizes, it shows that store’s name as a label. This app isn’t affiliated with any retailer it labels.

What happens when I delete or clear items?

Deleting an item removes it right away and shows Undo for a few seconds. Clear wishlist items in the Data menu empties your list the same way, with Undo. Clear all data asks you to type DELETE first and can’t be undone, so save a backup beforehand if you want one.

What is the sample data?

Seven sample items you can choose on the welcome screen to try the app. They never sync. Remove them with Remove sample data at the top of My list (or in Settings).

Keyboard shortcuts

Press / or Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac) to search, Ctrl+S (⌘S) to save a backup, and Esc to close a window.

Buying

How do I move something to Purchased?

Use the check mark next to an item’s edit and delete buttons, or Mark purchased in its edit window. Items without a link show a Mark purchased button instead of Buy now. Buy now only opens the store page, then asks if you bought it. To do several at once, tick them and use Buy selected.

What’s the “quick pause” when I mark something ready?

When a Want of $100 or more is set to “Ready to buy”, the app offers an optional waiting period of a day, a week or a month. Needs never get the prompt. During the wait the item shows a “Cooling off” label and Buy now asks once before opening the link. You can skip it every time.

What does “Not buying” do?

It takes an item off your wishlist without deleting it, and lists it under “Decided against” on the Purchased tab with what it would have cost. Items untouched for 90 days are flagged for a quick review so the list doesn’t fill up with things you no longer want.

What does the Share button make?

On the Purchased tab, Share makes a square image of your progress: how many planned things you bought, how many you decided against, and how many are still on your list. It’s drawn on your device and nothing is uploaded. Item names never go on it, and dollar amounts only appear if you turn them on.

Backups & moving

How do I back up or move my list to another device?

Open the Data menu. “Export All (JSON)” saves a full backup (including photos), “Export All (ZIP)” puts that backup, a spreadsheet and your photos in one file, and “Export All (CSV)” saves a spreadsheet copy. On the new device, use “Import Backup” and pick the JSON or ZIP file. Importing the same file twice won’t double anything. Or sign in to sync: your items then match on every device (photos stay on the device you added them on).

Where do backups get saved?

Wherever you choose. The first time you save, the browser asks for a folder and file name. After that, “Save backup now” (or Ctrl/Cmd+S) overwrites that same file. Firefox, Safari and phone browsers download to your Downloads folder instead.

Can I bring in my old spreadsheet?

Yes. Save it as CSV from Excel or Google Sheets (File, Save As or Download, CSV), then use Data, “Import Spreadsheet (CSV)”. The app finds the header row, guesses which column is the name, price, quantity, link, priority, category and date, and lets you change any of them before importing.

Install the app

Can I install it like an app?

Yes. In Chrome or Edge, use the Install button in Settings when your browser offers it, or the install icon in the address bar. On iPhone or iPad, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Installed, it opens in its own window and works offline after the first visit. On iPhone and iPad the Home Screen version keeps its own storage, so use a backup to move your list into it.

Your data & privacy

Where is my list saved?

Item details are saved in this browser’s local storage, and photos separately in IndexedDB on this device. Nothing is sent to a server unless you sign in to sync, and photos never are. Clearing browser data or using another browser shows an empty list, so keep a backup from the Data menu.

Does anyone else see my list?

No. Unless you sign in to sync, there’s no account and nothing leaves this browser. If you do sign in, your synced items can only be read by your own account. Details are in the .

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

wishlist.mcbflow.com is a free tool built by one person. You don't need an account, and nothing is sent to a server unless you choose to sign in to sync.

What's stored, and where

Item details are saved in local storage. Photos are saved in IndexedDB on your device. Backup file handles (when supported) are also kept in IndexedDB. Unless you sign in to sync, nothing is sent to a server.

Optional sync

If you sign in with a free mcbflow.com account (Data → Sync across devices), your items (names, prices and price history, links, notes, dates and status) are copied to your account so your other devices match. Photos are never uploaded; they stay on the device where you added them. Sample items, your theme, view and backup details stay on each device. The account database is run by Supabase (servers in the eastern United States); only your account can read your records. Your screen name and profile picture, if you choose them under Edit profile, are saved on your account so every mcbflow.com app shows the same ones. While signed in, this site also keeps your sign-in and a sync record (wishlist.sync) in local storage; with "Keep me signed in" turned off, the sign-in is kept in session storage instead and ends when the browser closes. Signing out stops syncing and keeps your list on this device. Export or delete synced data, or delete your account, on the mcbflow.com account page.

Keys used include: wishlist, wishlist.theme, wishlist.view, wishlist.lastBackup, wishlist.privacyAck, wishlist.onboarded, wishlist.keepSignedIn, and IndexedDB database wishlist.

To work offline, the app keeps a copy of its own page files (not your list) in the browser's Cache Storage. Spreadsheets you import are read in your browser and never uploaded.

Share images are drawn on your device. They go nowhere unless you save them or pick somewhere to share them, and they never include item names.
